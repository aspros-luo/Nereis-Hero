# Art Direction

## Target

Hand-painted 2D dark fantasy with graphic-novel readability.

## Principles

- Strong silhouettes.
- Restrained palette.
- Heavy environmental shadows.
- Organic corruption rather than generic gore.
- Human faces and body language remain important even when supernatural elements appear.
- Horror should often be implied before it is shown.

## Production approach

Prototype gameplay first with procedural/vector placeholder art. Replace high-value assets in this order:

1. Hero
2. First enemy
3. First environment
4. Mini-boss
5. Combat effects
6. NPCs and narrative props
