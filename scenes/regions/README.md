# Primary Region Entry Scenes

Canonical home for the A0–A13 campaign region entry scenes. Each entry scene is a real `.tscn` with its authored nodes visible in the Godot editor. Keep internal room scenes in their existing room directories; do not merge rooms into the region entry or generate scene-body nodes from scripts.

A0–A13 entries were moved here together with the known runtime, route-data, and acceptance references. `scenes/boot.tscn` remains the project launch scene, and the hidden-region, auxiliary, A2-room, and A5-room files remain in their existing directories.
